Research

Dissertation · 2026–27 · due March 2027

Adaptive Regime-aware Algorithm Selection for Multi-objective Delivery Routing with Demand Forecasting

Supervisor
Prof. Zidong Wang, Brunel University London
Question
When demand shifts between regimes (weekdays, seasonal peaks, holidays), does switching between multi-objective optimisers beat always using the single best one, once forecast error and the cost of switching are counted?
Approach
A two-layer system. Demand forecasts and clustered regimes decide which algorithm to run; the optimiser returns a Pareto front of routes trading off distance (cost) against lateness (customer satisfaction), on vehicle routing with time windows.
Algorithms
NSGA-II, NSGA-III, MOEA/D, SMS-EMOA
Progress
Pilot of 16,080 runs on 54 Solomon benchmark instances in MATLAB/PlatEMO. Early signs are that the benefit of switching may be narrow, and a clear negative result would still answer the question.
Evaluation
Against the best single algorithm on unseen data and held-out regimes, with a minimum improvement fixed in advance and statistical tests over repeated runs.
Tools
Python, MATLAB with PlatEMO
